# Storytelling procedure source

The former root-level storytelling draft used unratified shorthand such as `chapter`, `motive`,
and `clue`, and described session/recap behavior that does not yet have an owning feature.

The authoritative, catalog-seeded contract is now
[`procedure.play.storytelling`](catalog/procedures/play/procedure.play.storytelling.md). It uses
the canonical campaign/world identifiers and is the source to retrieve and follow during play.
