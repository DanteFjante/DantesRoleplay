Caldris is a grounded, low-magic D&D 2024-compatible setting spread across two continents and the
Lantern Sea. This index is the shortest path into the setting. All names, numbering, maps, and
images remain review content until a separately confirmed runtime preview and import boundary.

## Suggested reading routes

### Fifteen-minute overview

1. Read “Setting in one page” in the World bible.
2. Read the campaign promise and four enduring threads.
3. Open the World map and the Bramblebridge regional map in the visual pack.
4. Read Q01, **The Thirteenth Bell**, in the quest atlas.

### Prepare the opening sessions

1. Read Alderwick, Bramblebridge, Candlefen, and Wren's Hollow in the gazetteer.
2. Choose six to eight starting-region NPCs; keep Elowen, Nessa, Tibb, and Rusk available.
3. Read Volume I in the campaign tapestry.
4. Prepare Q01–Q05, but introduce only the hooks the party actually follows.
5. Keep the Gilded Kettle as a recurring place of food, gossip, recovery, and consequence.

### Build a longer campaign

1. Choose one Hearth thread, one Crown thread, and one Road thread.
2. Decide which Deep-history clue should first appear as an ordinary object or local custom.
3. Select one apparent-boss ladder and one genuinely unrelated adventure bridge.
4. Bring distant polities into focus only when travel, relationships, or consequences approach them.
5. Let defeated lower-layer antagonists stay defeated; the newly revealed layer must not erase the
   party's earlier achievement.

### Explore the second ring

1. Choose one expanded location reached through an existing road, relationship, trade link, or
   consequence.
2. Pair one local character with one visitor or correspondent from the earlier cast.
3. Add either a historical incident or living custom, but reveal it through work and evidence.
4. Select one story feature that lets an earlier choice reappear without predetermining its result.
5. Use Q31–Q48 as situations; freely detach their fresh doors when another direction fits better.

## Content inventory

- **World:** two continents, the Lantern Sea, twelve polities, thirty-six primary cities, seven
  historical eras, six known dragons, and twenty mythical-creature traditions.
- **People and institutions:** thirty-four factions, ninety-five local and polity NPC anchors, and
  four additional campaign-spine figures.
- **Stories:** four interwoven long-form threads, six campaign volumes, four explicit boss ladders,
  seventeen unrelated or sideways story bridges, forty reusable story features, and forty-eight
  prepared quest packets.
- **Places:** three deep starting settlements, twelve Bramblebridge playable sites, and thirty
  first-ring plus forty-eight second-ring settlements, landscapes, ruins, crossings, and adventure
  locations beyond the thirty-six primary cities.
- **History and lore:** seven broad eras, twenty-four additional incident dossiers, and twenty-four
  living customs, beliefs, and disputed memories with present-day uses.
- **Visuals:** nine generated concept images in a coherent watercolor-and-gouache storybook style.
- **Future work:** ninety-three candidate features, each separated into review-content opportunity
  or a retained runtime/authority gate.

## Authority boundary

This pack can guide discussion, review, session preparation, and later implementation design. It
does not create permanent IDs, D&D mechanics, canonical map coordinates, live World history,
player-visible secrets, runtime NPC relationships, item abilities, campaign state, or database
records. Those require the owners and confirmations named in the implementation map.
