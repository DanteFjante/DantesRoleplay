# Caldris additional cast — thirty-six second-ring characters

Status: **review content; characters are not runtime profiles or relationship state**
Existing cast: [Caldris cast and factions atlas](CALDRIS-CAST-AND-FACTIONS.md)
Locations: [Caldris expanded locations](CALDRIS-EXPANDED-LOCATIONS.md)
Slice: [Caldris Authoring Slice 2](CALDRIS-AUTHORING-SLICE-2-IMPLEMENTATION.md)

Each polity receives three recurring people. They are written as social engines rather than quest
dispensers: each has a manner, useful competence, immediate want, vulnerability, existing tie, and
a believable condition under which they change.

## Eredane

## Cast use rules

- Introduce a person through work, relationship, or immediate need before delivering biography.
- Let comic manner coexist with competence and private seriousness.
- A change trigger is an editorial possibility, not a guaranteed player-controlled switch.
- NPCs may disagree with or refuse the party without becoming villains.
- Runtime motives, knowledge, relationships, disposition, location, and change require their existing
  owners and player-safe projection gates.
