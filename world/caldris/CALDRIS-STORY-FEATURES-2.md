# Caldris story features — returns, relationships, and living consequences

Status: **forty review-content story features; not runtime mechanics**
Campaign: [The Measure of Mercy](CALDRIS-CAMPAIGN-TAPESTRY.md)
Cast: [Caldris additional cast](CALDRIS-ADDITIONAL-CAST.md)
Slice: [Caldris Authoring Slice 2](CALDRIS-AUTHORING-SLICE-2-IMPLEMENTATION.md)

These features are reusable authoring patterns. They help adventures feel connected without making
every event part of one master plot. None defines a numeric subsystem or autonomous state change.

## Relationships and recurring people

| Feature | How it appears in play | Later return | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Two-NPC promise** | Two NPCs make a promise to each other in the party's presence. | A later request tests the promise without making the party solely responsible. | Do not turn every promise into betrayal. |
| **Competence remembered** | An NPC notices the method the party used, not only success. | They ask for that expertise, teach it onward, or prepare around it. | Avoid typecasting one character forever. |
| **Earned teasing** | Familiar NPCs develop affectionate jokes based on shared events. | The tone changes naturally during danger or grief. | Never seize a player's vulnerability as a punchline. |
| **Friend of a friend** | Help given in one polity produces a cautious introduction elsewhere. | The introduction opens conversation, not automatic trust. | Reputation is specific, not universal currency. |
| **Rival grows sideways** | A rival improves in a different skill rather than merely matching power. | Cooperation becomes useful where specialties complement each other. | Do not erase rivalry or force friendship. |

## Home, downtime, and cozy returns

| Feature | How it appears in play | Later return | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Changing common room** | The Gilded Kettle gains a repair, menu item, guest, or inconvenience after each return. | Earlier choices become visible in ordinary routine. | Never destroy the refuge merely for stakes. |
| **Useful souvenir** | A mundane gift—recipe, cord, button, cup, seed—solves a later social or practical problem. | Its giver becomes present without appearing physically. | Keep it useful through context, not secret magic. |
| **Work beside talk** | Important conversations happen while mending nets, sorting grain, cooking, or walking animals. | NPCs reveal values through how they work. | Summarize labour that offers no choice. |
| **Seasonal table** | Food, clothing, light, prices, and routines change with the calendar. | Returning places feel alive rather than reset. | Season should create texture and choices, not constant penalties. |
| **Room kept, room changed** | A familiar room remains available but someone has altered one meaningful detail. | The detail signals welcome, grief, growth, or tension. | Do not narrate the player character's feelings about it. |

## Secrets, clues, and fair mysteries

| Feature | How it appears in play | Later return | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Three social distances** | A fact can be learned from an intimate, professional, or public source. | Different routes reveal different motives around the same fact. | Essential truth never depends on one NPC. |
| **Practical clue first** | Evidence matters to work before it matters to history: bad metal, bent hedge, fading ink. | The mundane solution remains valuable after the deeper reveal. | Do not make ordinary expertise merely decorative. |
| **Honest wrong witness** | A witness reports accurately but interprets through local belief or limited view. | Respectful comparison recovers truth without humiliating them. | Avoid “everyone local is superstitious” framing. |
| **Secret worth protecting** | The truth concerns safety, chosen identity, grief, or confidence rather than villainy. | Disclosure becomes an ethical choice with alternatives. | Concealment must not excuse ongoing serious harm. |
| **Clue that travels** | An object, phrase, tune, or custom reappears in another polity with altered meaning. | Comparison reveals historical contact or deliberate revision. | Not every repetition belongs to the central conspiracy. |

## Consequences and institutional memory

| Feature | How it appears in play | Later return | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Victory has paperwork** | After a rescue or exposure, people must repair titles, stores, schedules, or testimony. | A short follow-up lets players shape durable repair. | Administration supports consequence; it should not smother triumph. |
| **Lower boss stays beaten** | Removing a corrupt agent ends their actual harm. | A superior layer appears because records, vacancies, or frightened sponsors react. | Never reveal that the victory “didn't matter.” |
| **Beneficiary disagrees** | People helped by the party disagree about what should happen next. | Their debate creates politics without retroactive ingratitude. | Preserve recognition of what was improved. |
| **Institution learns unevenly** | One branch adopts reform while another resists or misunderstands it. | Similar future situations begin from changed conditions. | Avoid portraying institutions as one mind. |
| **Memorial choice** | A community chooses names, placement, ritual, and wording after loss. | The memorial becomes a future meeting place and historical source. | Grief is not a puzzle with one optimal answer. |

## Travel and roads with personality

| Feature | How it appears in play | Later return | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Road has a job** | A route exists for wool, pilgrimage, salt, rescue, cattle, or court travel. | Its users and maintenance explain encounters and conflicts. | Avoid random hazards detached from place. |
| **Shared shelter cast** | Weather gathers incompatible travelers under one roof. | One traveler later becomes ally, witness, rival, or correspondent. | Give players room to decline intimacy. |
| **News overtakes the party** | A faster courier carries an incomplete version of their deeds ahead. | Arrival begins with correction, leverage, or comic misconception. | Misreporting should be plausible, not punitive omniscience. |
| **Return route differs** | Season, repair, politics, or relationships alter a familiar journey. | Knowledge still helps even when circumstances change. | Do not invalidate maps or earlier learning arbitrarily. |
| **One quiet mile** | After danger, narration offers weather, landscape, food, or companionable silence. | Players may initiate conversation or simply travel. | Do not force emotional confession. |

## Humour with character roots

| Feature | How it appears in play | Later return | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ceremony meets furniture** | A grand ritual must fit a tiny room, low beam, stubborn chair, or muddy field. | Adaptation reveals who values purpose over appearance. | The tradition itself should retain meaning. |
| **Specialist enthusiasm** | An expert becomes delightfully intense about rope, jars, echoes, buttons, or ink. | Their niche later provides serious help. | Competence prevents eccentricity becoming mockery. |
| **Bad rehearsal, good heart** | Someone practices a speech, greeting, proposal, or heroic pose poorly. | In crisis they abandon polish and speak sincerely. | Do not require public humiliation. |
| **Animal with material motive** | A goat, rook, ox, mule, or cat disrupts events for food, habit, fear, or training. | Its behavior can become a fair clue. | Animals are not random gag machines. |
| **Bureaucratic courtesy duel** | Two officials compete through increasingly polite procedural obstacles. | A practical outsider or overlooked clause breaks the stalemate. | Keep officials individually motivated, not uniformly foolish. |

## Sadness, repair, and deeper events

| Feature | How it appears in play | Later return | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Loved before lost** | Establish a place, routine, craft, or relationship before serious harm threatens it. | Recovery preserves some specific valued element. | Never destroy care solely to demonstrate darkness. |
| **Different grief languages** | People cook, work, pray, withdraw, organize, joke later, or demand justice differently. | Reactions evolve rather than ending after one scene. | No single response is treated as morally mandatory. |
| **Repair cannot restore** | Players can prevent recurrence or return dignity without undoing death or exile. | The result becomes tradition, policy, or relationship. | Avoid hopelessness and cheap resurrection assumptions. |
| **Mercy has a witness** | A compassionate choice is observed by someone not yet important. | The witness later risks something because of what they learned. | Mercy does not guarantee loyalty or erase accountability. |
| **Joy after, not instead** | Celebration returns after grief has had space. | A changed song, recipe, game, or seat remembers the absent. | Do not use a joke to interrupt the serious choice itself. |

## Fresh doors and unrelated adventures

| Feature | How it appears in play | Later return | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Reward needs delivery** | Payment, thanks, or a gift must be carried somewhere personally. | The destination introduces a problem unrelated to the completed quest. | Delivery should be meaningful, not mandatory busywork. |
| **Celebration interruption without attack** | A cook vanishes, roof leaks, rival arrives, bell provenance is challenged, or weather changes. | The new adventure grows from ordinary life rather than ambush. | Let the prior victory be celebrated first. |
| **Wrong passenger, right boat** | Travel companions have their own bounded problem. | Helping them opens another region or relationship. | They are not secretly agents of the main villain by default. |
| **Professional referral** | An NPC recommends the party to someone facing a superficially similar but causally distinct issue. | Familiar skills meet a new moral context. | Avoid repeating the same solution. |
| **Coincidental object with provenance** | A market purchase or inherited item has a human history, not necessarily magical power. | Following it reveals family, labour, migration, or old law. | Coincidence opens a door; it does not solve the mystery. |

## Feature boundary

These patterns guide authored scenes and later review. Durable relationships, clocks, state changes,
reputation, secrets, travel, inventory, rewards, and consequences require their existing runtime
owners and must never be inferred from prose alone.
