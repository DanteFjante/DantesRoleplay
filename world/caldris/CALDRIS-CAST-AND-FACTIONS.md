# Caldris cast and factions atlas

Status: **expanded review content; interaction cards are not runtime profiles**
World context: [Caldris world bible](CALDRIS-WORLD-BIBLE.md)
Place context: [Caldris gazetteer](CALDRIS-GAZETTEER.md)

This atlas supplies thirty-four factions, fifteen detailed starting-region NPCs, forty-four wider
polity anchors, and a recurring campaign-spine cast. Each person has a playable contrast rather
than a single comic trait. Editorial relationships must later map to confirmed World/campaign
owners before becoming live state.

## Fifteen detailed starting-region NPCs

### Magistrate Elowen Pike — strict, fair, and privately untidy

- **Role:** Bramblebridge magistrate, seal holder, first serious civic ally.
- **Immediate want:** restore lawful toll practice without provoking a riot or humiliating honest
  junior officials.
- **Long want:** prove small-town justice can be both rigorous and humane.
- **Competence:** evidence, procedure, interviewing, remembering exact promises.
- **Vulnerability:** takes responsibility for systemic failures personally and sleeps badly.
- **Public face / contradiction:** immaculate hearings; chaotic kitchen garden and five unfinished
  letters of apology.
- **Kindness:** quietly pays travel costs for witnesses who would otherwise lose wages.
- **Boundary:** will not accept mind influence, planted evidence, or punishment without hearing.
- **Voice:** short sentences, exact verbs, unexpectedly dry jokes delivered without warning.
- **Knows/suspects/hides:** knows the toll orders conflict; suspects higher authorization; hides that
  her late father once accepted a Concord exemption for the family bridge plot.
- **Change:** becomes flexible when players bring reliable alternatives, harsher if they ask her to
  ignore harm for convenience.

### Nessa Quill — laid-back ferrymaster who misses very little

- **Role:** Candlefen ferry operator, flood expert, gossip broker, marsh guide.
- **Immediate want:** stop drainage investors from narrowing a flood channel.
- **Long want:** train two successors without forcing either child to inherit her life.
- **Competence:** boats, weather, people under pressure, reading water by debris.
- **Vulnerability:** avoids direct conflict until delay becomes costly.
- **Public face / contradiction:** jokes that everything can wait; keeps meticulous flood notebooks.
- **Kindness:** dry socks, tea, and no questions until a person is warm.
- **Boundary:** will not guide armed strangers into the drowned groves without local consent.
- **Voice:** slow, amused, uses questions as answers, becomes startlingly crisp in danger.
- **Knows/suspects/hides:** knows a submerged route; suspects the drainage plan benefits smugglers;
  hides one sighting of Mourenne from twenty years ago.
- **Change:** offers deeper knowledge after players protect people without treating fen life as
  quaint ignorance.

### Tibb Fallow — cringeworthy poet-watchman and excellent map memory

- **Role:** junior watch officer, amateur romantic poet, unexpected route specialist.
- **Immediate want:** impress Magistrate Pike with a heroic arrest and Fenna Dorr with a sonnet.
- **Long want:** be respected for more than enthusiasm.
- **Competence:** remembers maps after one viewing, runs well, notices altered signs.
- **Vulnerability:** talks past social cues and embellishes before he realizes he is doing it.
- **Public face / contradiction:** self-declared “Falcon of the Weir”; terrified of geese.
- **Kindness:** escorts drunk, lost, or embarrassed townspeople home without recording it.
- **Boundary:** refuses to falsify a report even when truth makes him look ridiculous.
- **Voice:** rehearsed grandeur collapsing into earnest detail.
- **Knows/suspects/hides:** knows every local path; suspects the new patrol map is wrong; hides that
  a romantic gift map accidentally includes a smuggling trail.
- **Change:** grows into genuine confidence if trusted with meaningful work rather than mocked.

### Brother Odo Mallow — eccentric beekeeper, mediocre singer, patient historian

- **Role:** Mallow Abbey apiary keeper and assistant archivist.
- **Immediate want:** understand why bees built comb around an erased charter name.
- **Long want:** preserve local oral memories alongside formal records.
- **Competence:** bees, plants, old hands, listening to elderly witnesses.
- **Vulnerability:** becomes fascinated at socially terrible times.
- **Public face / contradiction:** cheerful distraction; quietly carries grief from a failed healing
  during the Summer Without Swallows.
- **Kindness:** remembers how everyone takes porridge and never mentions hardship directly.
- **Boundary:** will not destroy a living hive for faster evidence.
- **Voice:** gentle digressions that eventually prove relevant.
- **Knows/suspects/hides:** knows the erased family name; suspects the abbey profited from the
  erasure; hides his former connection to a court magical college.
- **Change:** risks institutional censure if the party treats truth and people with equal care.

### Merrit Vale — cheerful meddling innkeeper with a long memory

- **Role:** owner of the Gilded Kettle, social centre, provider of food and unsolicited introductions.
- **Immediate want:** identify who sends letters to the burned Green Ladle inn.
- **Long want:** ensure the Kettle survives without trapping their niece in succession.
- **Competence:** hospitality, accounts, reading moods, finding someone a useful seatmate.
- **Vulnerability:** confuses helpfulness with permission.
- **Public face / contradiction:** open book; keeps confidences with near-professional discipline.
- **Kindness:** feeds first, negotiates payment later, maintains a quiet room for overwhelmed guests.
- **Boundary:** no violence in the common room and no humiliating someone who cannot leave.
- **Voice:** fast, warm, names everyone twice, whispers only when unnecessary.
- **Knows/suspects/hides:** knows most town relationships; suspects Rusk's ring; hides that Rusk once
  saved the inn from a fire.
- **Change:** learns to ask before arranging lives if players confront the behaviour kindly.

### Della Crookshaw — gentle grump and master miller

- **Role:** Old Weir mill owner, employer, flour supplier, mechanical witness.
- **Immediate want:** explain the nightly wheel stoppage without closing the mill during harvest.
- **Long want:** transfer ownership to the workers without losing her family's home.
- **Competence:** machinery, river flow, bargaining, detecting bad grain by smell.
- **Vulnerability:** expresses fear as criticism.
- **Public face / contradiction:** complains constantly; forgives debts quietly.
- **Kindness:** keeps a flour account for households who cannot pay.
- **Boundary:** will not risk workers for curiosity.
- **Voice:** inventive complaints, precise technical language, affection disguised as insult.
- **Knows/suspects/hides:** knows someone enters the sluice; suspects sabotage; hides a deed problem.
- **Change:** respects creative plans that include worker safety and maintenance.

### Fenna Dorr — exacting guild assessor and enthusiastic puppet-maker

- **Role:** Honest Weights inspector, evidence ally, Tibb's awkward crush.
- **Immediate want:** find how a hollow standard entered the hall.
- **Long want:** reform certification so travelling traders can appeal locally.
- **Competence:** measures, metal, handwriting, quiet cross-examination.
- **Vulnerability:** mistakes emotional reserve for clarity.
- **Public face / contradiction:** severe assessor; performs absurd puppet voices for children.
- **Kindness:** teaches apprentices to challenge senior mistakes safely.
- **Boundary:** no unverified public accusation.
- **Voice:** formal in public, unexpectedly silly with trusted people.
- **Knows/suspects/hides:** knows the standard was switched; suspects guild leadership; hides a
  family connection to a Seventh Ledger clerk.
- **Change:** accepts intuition as a lead after it produces testable evidence.

### Fara Dint — strict toll inspector caught in someone else's design

- **Role:** unpopular new official and potential witness rather than simple villain.
- **Immediate want:** meet impossible revenue targets without losing her post.
- **Long want:** earn a transfer to Merecross and support two dependent siblings.
- **Competence:** forms, counting, detecting casual evasion.
- **Vulnerability:** obeys written authority beyond common sense when frightened.
- **Public face / contradiction:** officious and humourless; writes devastating comic marginalia in
  private.
- **Kindness:** never confiscates essential medicine even when ordered.
- **Boundary:** refuses bribery but accepts bad orders as legitimate.
- **Voice:** regulation numbers, corrected grammar, suppressed exasperation.
- **Knows/suspects/hides:** knows exemptions benefit specific carriers; suspects Provost Drail;
  hides a threat against her siblings.
- **Change:** can become a rigorous reform ally if protected and held accountable without cruelty.

### Jory Fenner — warm rogue, tanner, and small-scale smuggler

- **Role:** leatherworker, informal broker, route contact.
- **Immediate want:** keep medicine and tools moving around predatory tolls.
- **Long want:** become legitimate without betraying people who relied on him.
- **Competence:** disguises, leatherwork, bargaining, hidden compartments.
- **Vulnerability:** overestimates his ability to control criminal partners.
- **Public face / contradiction:** charming cynic; gives exact change and remembers favours.
- **Kindness:** repairs children's shoes free and pretends the scraps were worthless.
- **Boundary:** no people trafficking, addictive poison, or violence against inspectors' families.
- **Voice:** easy jokes, flattering questions, becomes plain when afraid.
- **Knows/suspects/hides:** knows Rusk's routes; suspects higher patronage; hides his role in one
  disastrous diversion.
- **Change:** turns witness or faction founder depending on how players treat lawful versus moral
  responsibility.

### Sera Lark — dramatic theatre director with practical courage

- **Role:** leader of Bramble Theatre Loft, rumour amplifier, disguise source.
- **Immediate want:** recover a villain costume containing a real ministerial dispatch.
- **Long want:** stage a touring history cycle that does not flatter the Crown.
- **Competence:** voices, crowd reading, logistics, sewing, distraction.
- **Vulnerability:** frames conflict theatrically and sometimes escalates it.
- **Public face / contradiction:** extravagant; meticulously pays workers before patrons.
- **Kindness:** gives shy people backstage work without forcing performance.
- **Boundary:** will not use private grief as public material without consent.
- **Voice:** vivid metaphors, stage directions in conversation, sincere apologies.
- **Knows/suspects/hides:** knows who planted the dispatch; suspects censorship; hides that the
  costume came from Rusk.
- **Change:** becomes a continental information ally if the troupe travels.

### Archivist Nan Wimple — irritable guardian of local memory

- **Role:** lay archivist at Mallow Abbey, Odo's professional foil.
- **Immediate want:** stabilize damaged charters before enthusiastic investigators touch them.
- **Long want:** train a successor who values both order and access.
- **Competence:** provenance, ink, institutional history, identifying later insertions.
- **Vulnerability:** uses control to manage fear of loss.
- **Public face / contradiction:** territorial; leaves anonymous copies where poor claimants will
  find them.
- **Kindness:** reads records aloud to anyone who cannot read.
- **Boundary:** no removal of originals without a witnessed emergency.
- **Voice:** clipped, specific, devastating sighs.
- **Knows/suspects/hides:** knows the erased name and the abbey's benefit; hides that she forged a
  copy years ago to protect the original.
- **Change:** shares deeper access after careful evidence handling.

### Rusk Pettifer — theatrical smuggler and apparent first boss

- **Role:** organizer of toll evasion, coercion, and forged exemptions.
- **Immediate want:** make the new toll system unworkable enough that his network becomes essential.
- **Long want:** gain respectable control of regional freight.
- **Competence:** logistics, charm, reading ambition, nonlethal contingency planning.
- **Vulnerability:** believes gratitude creates permanent loyalty.
- **Public face / contradiction:** elegant criminal patron; genuinely protected Bramblebridge during
  a past flood when officials failed.
- **Kindness:** pays widows reliably, partly from principle and partly from branding.
- **Boundary:** claims to avoid killing; his pressure methods still put people at risk.
- **Voice:** polished compliments, theatrical disappointment, never raises his voice until plans fail.
- **Knows/suspects/hides:** knows Provost Drail sponsors selective chaos; suspects a ministerial
  network; hides how frightened he is of being made disposable.
- **Change:** can be defeated, exposed, bargained with, displaced, or turned witness; none erases
  the real good or harm he did.

### Arlen Pott — rival adventurer and overprepared worrier

- **Role:** captain of the Wayward Company.
- **Immediate want:** complete the contract accidentally assigned to both companies.
- **Long want:** become famous enough to retire her parents' debts.
- **Competence:** planning, climbing, first aid, making camps genuinely comfortable.
- **Vulnerability:** prepares for spectacular dangers and misses mundane motives.
- **Public face / contradiction:** confident captain; carries twelve emergency lists.
- **Kindness:** shares supplies immediately, complains about reimbursement later.
- **Boundary:** will not abandon another party in danger, even a rival.
- **Voice:** brisk plans interrupted by contingency footnotes.
- **Knows/suspects/hides:** knows a League courier route; suspects the party resents her; hides that
  her contract is financially ruinous.
- **Change:** becomes comic rival, trusted ally, or principled critic rather than a recurring loser.

### Pella Sorn — travelling repairer and quietly intense craftsperson

- **Role:** pot, buckle, hinge, and small clock repairer; item provenance contact.
- **Immediate want:** identify the maker of a bellglass compass.
- **Long want:** recover her mentor's travelling workshop, seized for debt.
- **Competence:** metals, mechanisms, recognizing repairs and tool marks.
- **Vulnerability:** finds people harder to read than objects.
- **Public face / contradiction:** reserved; expresses affection through perfectly timed repairs.
- **Kindness:** fixes one essential household object free in every village.
- **Boundary:** refuses to reproduce coercive devices or false seals.
- **Voice:** sparse, literal, unexpectedly intense about hinges.
- **Knows/suspects/hides:** recognizes Concord workmanship; suspects the compass is future-dated;
  hides that her mentor joined the Seventh Ledger voluntarily.
- **Change:** follows the party across regions when item mysteries deepen.

### Curdin “Curd” Bale — cheerful cheesemonger and useful coward

- **Role:** market gossip, cart owner, witness who fled at the right time.
- **Immediate want:** retrieve a cheese cart seized under impossible poultry regulations.
- **Long want:** win Bellafont's regional cheese medal without visiting Bellafont.
- **Competence:** faces, road news, food preservation, strategic exits.
- **Vulnerability:** fear makes him omit important facts until he feels safe.
- **Public face / contradiction:** comic complainer; remembers exactly who helped during hardship.
- **Kindness:** feeds watch prisoners because “innocence is no excuse for bad cheese.”
- **Boundary:** will not knowingly put family in danger.
- **Voice:** culinary analogies of escalating specificity.
- **Knows/suspects/hides:** saw the empty tax wagon arrive; suspects the driver left willingly;
  hides that he transported one unlisted passenger.
- **Change:** becomes a reliable courier if treated as cautious rather than contemptible.

## Campaign-spine figures above the first apparent boss

### Provost Halwen Drail — the superior who calls exploitation reform

Regional revenue provost, severe, controlled, and convinced that predictable extraction is kinder
than arbitrary local privilege. Drail sponsors selective toll chaos so favored carriers and new
central offices appear necessary. Rusk believes Drail is the top of the network. Drail actually
receives policy packages from Chancellor Sera Vane's office and resents the implied supervision.

Defeating Drail may mean public proof, removal, forced testimony, loss of patronage, or a negotiated
reform. Drail's fall exposes higher authority but still matters: regional coercion decreases and
several honest officials are freed to act.

### Chancellor Sera Vane — the higher official who wants a stronger peace

Queen Adela's Chancellor of Roads and Revenue, composed, funny in private, and increasingly willing
to manipulate law. Vane fears a succession crisis will break the Lantern Sea Accord. She uses old
Concord methods to centralize tolls, messages, and emergency authority, believing temporary control
can prevent war.

Vane is not formally a member of the Seventh Ledger. Her confidential adviser is. She can become an
antagonist, reluctant ally, deposed reformer, or architect of a less coercive system depending on
what the party proves and what alternatives they build.

### Auditor Celyn Marr — the sponsor above the minister

A pleasant, forgettable policy adviser who never carries a weapon and rarely tells a direct lie.
Celyn leads a Seventh Ledger restoration cell and supplied Vane with convincing analyses, forged
precedents, and real evidence of approaching instability. Celyn views people as entries that should
be made legible for their own protection.

Celyn answers to a Council of Margins distributed across both continents. The Council itself is
divided: restorationists want Concord authority, custodians want dangerous archives contained, and
reformers want public standards without inherited debt. “The boss” becomes a political network,
not an infinite line of stronger combatants.

### Ilyr Vaust — the supposed master who withdrew centuries ago

The Seventh Ledger uses Ilyr's old mark and fragments of the dragon's early Concord advice. Many
members believe the Brass Archivist guides them through intermediaries. Ilyr does not. Discovering
this does not make the dragon innocent: Ilyr helped build the original system, abandoned people who
depended on it, and kept evidence that could have changed later history.

Ilyr is a final-scale moral and historical confrontation, but not necessarily a combat boss. The
party may demand testimony, expose the dragon, steal or negotiate archive access, repair an ancient
wrong, or reject the idea that any single keeper should own the past.

## Cast use rules

- A comic first impression never eliminates competence, harm, grief, or capacity to change.
- Apparent bosses have superiors or sponsors where appropriate, but each victory changes material
  conditions and is never declared meaningless by the next reveal.
- Not every faction or NPC connects to the Seventh Ledger. Independent people and problems keep the
  World from collapsing into one conspiracy.
- Runtime creation must later separate narrative identity, motive, knowledge, faction ties,
  creature/stat-block binding, disposition, and campaign consequence through their actual owners.
