# Ganji character integration — the Unbidden Eye

Status: **reviewed character-linked campaign content**
World: Caldris
Campaign: *The Measure of Mercy*
Character status: narrative concept approved; executable D&D character not yet created

## Ganji

Ganji is a young monk who was born with catlike eyes, ears, balance, and reflexes. His shepherd
village in the Button Hills surrendered him as an infant to the Tensor Sect after treating those
traits as an omen. The sect revered his natural gifts while denying him an ordinary childhood.
They named him **the Unbidden Eye** and subjected him to increasingly strict training because they
believed he might achieve omniscient perception.

Ganji eventually escaped the sect at night. He seeks a purpose he can choose and a form of devotion
he can genuinely feel. He wants to become stronger, but refuses to remain someone else's sacred
instrument.

### Character foundations

- **Ideal:** Devotion must be chosen. Obedience without love is captivity.
- **Bond:** Ganji sympathizes with people trapped by duties that others call honourable.
- **Flaw:** Praise for his gifts makes him suspicious because admiration has often become ownership.
- **Fear:** The sect may be right that he has a unique destiny, and leaving may have endangered
  people who tried to protect him.
- **Immediate desire:** Experience ordinary life, form relationships without prophecy attached,
  and find a purpose worth choosing.
- **Long question:** Can Ganji become powerful without becoming another person's instrument?

His Tensor training supports a future D&D 2024 Monk focused on agility, perception, insight,
stealth, and anticipating movement. It grants no executable species trait, supernatural prediction,
class feature, proficiency, score, or rule result until the governed character-creation mechanic
records those choices.

## The Tensor Sect

The Tensor Sect is a small Highmead philosophical order. Its monks study the instant before action:
breath, balance, gaze, muscle tension, and the displacement of weight. They teach that every motion
is joined to every other motion by invisible tension. A sufficiently disciplined observer might
perceive the whole pattern at once and see the divine source that Caldris's Nine Lamps only refract.

The sect's belief in Ganji is sincere and coercive. Some monks love him, some envy him, and many
cannot distinguish protecting him from owning his future. His escape has divided the order between
those who want him returned, those who want him free, and those who think his successful flight is
the sect's greatest revelation.

## Locations

### House of the Ninth Angle

A severe chalk monastery built into Highmead's Whitewall district. Uneven floors, angled mirrors,
sparring galleries, and observation cells make unconscious habits visible. The monastery feeds
travellers and teaches excellent first aid, but its internal discipline grants senior teachers
troubling authority over novices.

### The Prediction Court

An interior court crossed by lines of suspended bronze bells. Students must move through without
ringing them while instructors introduce distractions. Ganji crossed the entire court during his
escape without sounding a bell. The sect has covered and preserved his final footprints as a sacred
and politically inconvenient mystery.

### The Button Hills

Ganji's birthplace remains uncertain within the existing chalk sheep country. Several pages naming
his family were removed from the sect's intake folio. At least one villager who participated in
surrendering him has regretted it ever since.

## Character-linked quest hooks

### Ganji Hook — The Hands That Retrieve

Three Tensor monks arrive in Bramblebridge with travel papers describing Ganji as a protected
novice rather than a fugitive. Their leader sincerely loves him but refuses to recognise his right
to leave. The conflict may be handled through evasion, public law, negotiation, exposure of the
sect's custody practices, or a voluntary test that Ganji defines for himself.

### Ganji Hook — The Missing Folio

Pages from Ganji's childhood intake record appear among documents exposed by a Button Hills
sinkhole. They suggest the sect knew more about his family and unusual traits than it admitted.
Recovering the complete history requires protecting fragile evidence and deciding whether the
people who surrendered him deserve privacy, confrontation, forgiveness, or none of those things.

### Ganji Hook — The Bell Without Motion

A Tensor instructor disappears while investigating a bell reported to ring before anyone touches
its rope. The incident may be fraud, an application of exceptional perception, or evidence that a
foundational Tensor teaching arose from a misunderstood physical phenomenon. Ganji can investigate
without accepting the sect's claim that every discovery belongs to it.

## Opening connection

Ganji reaches Bramblebridge shortly before the opening of *The Thirteenth Bell*. When the apparently
driverless tax wagon enters the rainy market, his training makes crowd movement, the horse's
attention, and one bystander's premature reaction natural things for him to examine. What he notices
and whether uncertainty requires a check remain decisions for the live D&D rules owner.
