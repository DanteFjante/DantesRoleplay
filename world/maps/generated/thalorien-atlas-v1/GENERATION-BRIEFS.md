# Thalorien atlas v1 generation briefs

Status: **candidate assets awaiting user approval; not linked to runtime state**

## Shared brief

Use case: `stylized-concept`. Asset type: fantasy RPG world or regional map base art. Match the
accepted world pass with premium hand-painted fantasy cartography, painterly realism, engraved ink
contours, watercolor and gouache terrain, and old-world atlas craftsmanship. Use a landscape 4:3
composition, top-down oblique relief, north at top, and a public-player-safe unlabelled base suitable
for UI overlays. No text, labels, letters, legend, grid, political borders, UI, watermark, decorative
creatures or ships, canonical roads, route lines, caves, portals, dungeons, hidden sites, treasure,
hazards, tactical geometry, or invented settlements. Existing named elements may appear only as
small unlabelled illustrative silhouettes. Painted terrain remains illustrative and non-authoritative.

## World pass

Regenerate the known continent of Thalos as one coherent organic continent while preserving the
existing map's north/south/east/west ordering. Keep Evandos north, Minevros northwest, Rhiannos
northeast, Aldros central and landlocked, Waylos southwest, Merceros south, Valeros southeast, and
the neutral World Tree Grounds at the absolute center. Include only public established geography:
Northern Cold Highlands; northeastern snow, mountains, and forest; the World Tree and Elven enclave;
the southern trade coast; Mount Cinderwake and the Ashen Crown Isles; Valeros's Greenmantle,
Oathshield Range, agricultural belt, tropical cliffs, and jungle valleys; and subtle established
settlement/tower silhouettes.

## Regional passes

- **Aldros:** gentle cultivated central lowlands, World Tree Grounds adjoining the kingdom,
  Crownmere east of the tree, Larkspire University west; no coastline or dramatic mountain ring.
- **Evandos:** Northern Cold Highlands, snowy far north, ruined Harrowfall beside the highlands,
  warmer transition toward central Thalos.
- **Minevros:** rugged northwestern coast, restrained uplands and wooded valleys, subtle Starwell
  Tower devoted to scholarship and observation.
- **Rhiannos:** heavily forested northeast, cold and snowy far north, subtle Dawnbell Tower associated
  with healing and refuge.
- **Valeros:** Greenmantle in the west; Brackenford beside a separate smaller ancient forest;
  Oathshield Range and ruined Bannerfall Tower in the southeast; northern agricultural belt; high
  tropical cliffs and jungle valleys farther southeast.
- **Merceros:** broad southern trade coast and cultivated interior, with Merrowgate as the one
  established harbor city; no magic tower.
- **Waylos:** dry ash-colored southwestern country, Mount Cinderwake, offshore Ashen Crown hints,
  and the subtle active Emberwright Tower near the volcanic region.
- **Southwestern Volcanic Region:** Mount Cinderwake on the mainland plus the separate offshore Ashen
  Crown Isles with black beaches and fertile green slopes.
- **World Tree Grounds:** the approximately 2,000-meter sacred World Tree and Elaris, the largest
  Elven settlement, integrated around its base in neutral shared territory.

## Visual review notes

The first Aldros pass was rejected for contradicting the established lowland character with an
excessive mountain ring. The first Merceros pass was rejected because it inherited a non-canonical
tower from the world reference. Neither rejected image was copied into this candidate atlas.
