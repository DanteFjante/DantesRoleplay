## Description

Owns explicit creature Condition instances and the one administrative mutation path. It stores no
derived D20, action-economy, movement, damage, death, duration, cause, or history state.

## Instructions

1. Source: `dnd2024.source.srd-5.2.1`, `Rules Glossary`, including the fifteen named Conditions and
   the cumulative `Exhaustion` Condition.
2. Keep one closed, canonically sorted component. Non-Exhaustion instances are unique by Condition
   and optional source entity; one source-free Exhaustion entry carries level 1–6.
3. `record` creates known-empty state. `apply` and `clear` handle explicit non-Exhaustion IDs.
   `exhaust` and `recover` change the aggregate level. Every successful transition writes one
   complete component and fixes provenance.
4. Charmed, Frightened, and Grappled require a non-self source. Petrified removes Poisoned in the
   same transition; Poisoned cannot be applied while Petrified is effective.
5. Level-six Exhaustion remains canonical lethal state and is reported in the transition result;
   death-state mutation belongs to a separately accepted owner.

## Constraints

Callers never provide source IDs, provenance, entries, causes, effective Conditions, derived values,
events, or effects. Reject corrupt state, duplicates, unavailable clears, unsupported IDs,
self/missing required sources, invalid Exhaustion changes, and Petrified/Poisoned conflicts without
repair or partial mutation.
