## Description

Provides a deterministic read-only view of physical items beneath one custody root.

## Instructions

Inspect the declared four-level containment projection and surface both physical items and visible
unclassified contents in pre-order.

## Constraints

The view is bounded, proposes no effects, and infers no owner, accessibility, capacity, value,
weight, or permission. Corrupt visible item state fails closed.
