## Description

Records whether one directly possessed eligible physical item is held, worn, or unequipped.

## Instructions

Equip only a directly held item in a definition-permitted mode; read without effects; unequip before
ordinary transfer.

## Constraints

Containment remains custody. Fungible stacks cannot equip, and equipped items must be unequipped
before transfer. Slots, hand counts, AC, attacks, don/doff cost, and action resources are separate.
