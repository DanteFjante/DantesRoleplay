## Description

Use this procedure when the DM or authorized AI has already selected a current attitude and wants
to establish the transition, reasons, and any separate campaign consequence as authoritative state.

## Instructions

Bind the exact source actor and target actor or faction. Omit `relationship` only for the initial
record; later changes must bind the current record and its exact internal revision. Supply one or
more reason facts for an initial or changed attitude. A verified conversation, negotiation, or
ability-check receipt may be cited by its immutable receipt ID, but it never selects the attitude.

Use the same next attitude as the current attitude with no new reasons, evidence, consequence, or
visibility change for a true no-op. Corrections are explicit later transitions with provenance;
historical `dnd2024.social.attitude-changed` events are never erased.

## Constraints

The mechanic records a caller-selected transition only. It must not evaluate persuasion, invent
feelings or speech, decide acceptance, or predict an NPC action. Reason and consequence prose is
stored in visibility-bearing generic facts and is returned only through the host-audience-bound
`dnd2024.query.social-context` projection.
