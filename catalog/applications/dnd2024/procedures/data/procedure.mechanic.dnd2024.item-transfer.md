## Description

Moves one whole physical item from its declared direct source to an explicit destination after
checking published direct item-count, weight, and permitted-kind capacity.

## Instructions

Bind the item, its exact direct source, and destination, then supply only the new direct slot.

## Constraints

Stack splitting is separate. Missing/malformed facts fail closed. Direct-container admission does
not imply carrying or ancestor capacity. Visible cycles are refused and the containment writer is
the final cycle authority.
