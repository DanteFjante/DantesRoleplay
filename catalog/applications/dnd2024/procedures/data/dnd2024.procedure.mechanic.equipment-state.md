## Description

Records who has equipped one eligible quantity-one item and which canonical equipment slots it
occupies. Absence of equipment state means unequipped.

## Instructions

Equip only an item directly contained by the declared holder. Pass `slotIds` that occur in the
definition's `equipmentSlots`; read without effects; unequip by removing equipment state before
ordinary transfer. Definitions with an unevaluated equip requirement or equipped effect must use
their owning rule flow rather than this primitive.

## Constraints

Containment remains custody. Only quantity `1` can equip, and equipped items must be unequipped
before transfer. Hand occupancy beyond selected slots, AC, attacks, don/doff cost, and action
resources are separate.
