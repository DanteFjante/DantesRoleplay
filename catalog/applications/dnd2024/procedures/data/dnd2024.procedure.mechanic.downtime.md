## Description

Governs the common authoritative lifecycle for bounded D&D 2024 downtime without making creative
decisions for the DM or AI.

## Instructions

1. Author one immutable definition with a review-assigned revision and content fingerprint. Select
   exactly one family: crafting, recovery, service, training, or lifestyle.
2. Declare the exact duration, prerequisite keys, physical reservation totals, provider/location
   requirements, and one cancellation policy. Omitted behavior is forbidden, never inferred.
3. Call `downtime.begin` with selected physical stacks beneath the participant. The mechanic checks
   totals, consumes them once, and records their refund evidence atomically.
4. Call `downtime.progress` with the current clock revision. It advances both activity and world
   clock once; partial progress remains active and exact duration becomes ready.
5. Call `downtime.complete` only when ready. Crafting binds the exact authored output definition and
   delegates creation to `item-instance.create-and-place`. Other families record mechanical
   completion and leave all creative outcomes to the DM or AI.
6. Call `downtime.cancel` only when the authored policy permits. Retain keeps reservations; refund
   requires explicit replacement IDs and recreates exactly what begin consumed; forbid requires a
   separately authorized compensating transaction.
7. Use `dnd2024.query.downtime-status` before acting. Returned next actions expose known roles and
   inputs, missing values, stale definition or clock evidence, and whether the action is ready.
8. Retries must reuse the original idempotency key. Completed and cancelled history remains pinned
   to its definition fingerprint even after later definitions are retired.
