## Description

Returns a bounded, audience-filtered summary of poison, contagion, curse, and environmental
exposure records already established for one subject.

## Instructions

Bind the exact audience-authorized subject and active world, then call
`dnd2024.query.hazard-status`. The projection follows only the declared incoming subject
relationships and evaluates recorded due minutes against the authoritative clock. It does not
scan the whole world, search for traps, infer an exposure, or establish a new affliction.

Player results omit concealed curse applications without revealing their identities or count. DM
results may include them. Both perspectives receive only established application identity,
lifecycle status, pinned definition evidence, bounded progress, and due status. Use the focused
lifecycle mechanic for any change.

## Constraints

- A status projection returns no effects, events, or notifications.
- Missing or malformed related records fail closed instead of being summarized approximately.
- Concealed curse identity never appears in a player result, count, narration, or source reference.
- The query does not decide that a trap was detected or that a hazard affected a subject.
