## Description

Use focused trap mechanics for detection, disarming, triggering, resetting, and clearing. Bind the
exact trap instance, immutable definition entity, authored check activity, actor or target, current
definition revision, and the active mechanic fingerprint. Never derive a DC or consequence from
narrative text.

## Instructions

1. Use `dnd2024.mechanic.trap.detect` for an unknown trap. Supply its exact authored detection
   activity as `activity` and put only the ability-check object under `check`.
2. A failed detection response deliberately omits the trap identity and details. Do not add it to
   player context, logs, next actions, or previews.
3. Use `dnd2024.mechanic.trap.disarm` only after the actor has the `dnd2024.hazard.detected`
   relationship to the trap.
4. Use `dnd2024.mechanic.trap.trigger` with damage and Condition arrays copied from the authored
   allowlist and adjusted only by the verified save result.
5. Use reset only after its authored reset condition is externally established. Use clear to end
   active play without deleting the definition or history.

## Constraints

- Empty production trap effects or missing detection/disarm activities are incomplete authored
  content and must fail closed.
- Only the declared hazard-damage and Conditions mechanics are valid consequence owners.
- Reuse one idempotency key for retries; never resubmit a successful lifecycle action as new work.
