## Description

Begin, progress, resolve, and recover one definition-linked environmental exposure through exact
public action contracts. Progress owns one authored interval and advances the authoritative clock
in the same transaction as the verified check, state, consequences, and event.

## Instructions

1. Call `dnd2024.mechanic.environment-exposure.begin` with a new exposure ID, subject, exact hazard
   definition, and optional pending travel schedule from Slice 9.
2. Call `dnd2024.mechanic.environment-exposure.progress` for exactly one authored minute interval.
   Bind the exact check activity, even when an authorized DM or AI selects an authored mitigation.
3. Supply `selectedMitigationIndex` only when that indexed authored mitigation is established.
   Otherwise supply the exact authored check and consequence arrays.
4. Call recover when recovery is established. The record and events remain as provenance, while
   later progress is rejected.

## Constraints

- Missing cadence, check owner, closed consequence allowlist, or audience-safe role binding is a
  stop condition, not permission to invent rules.
- Vectors or narrative text cannot establish mitigation, permission, or consequence truth.
- Retry with the original idempotency key so neither the interval nor the clock advances twice.
