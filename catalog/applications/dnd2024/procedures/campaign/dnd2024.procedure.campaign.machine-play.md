## Description

This procedure teaches an AI to simulate reviewed campaign play through multiple chapters while
keeping rules, World state, campaign continuity, and word-for-word conversation evidence separate.

## Instructions

1. Orient, resolve the host-bound application and state space, then read `procedure.system.use`,
   `dnd2024.procedure.campaign.bootstrap-play`, `dnd2024.procedure.campaign.party`, and this contract.
2. Inspect the exact campaign, active session, active arc, active chapter, current scene, World clock,
   party participations, destination locations, and relevant authored facts. Never derive hidden
   information from filenames or unbound catalog records.
3. Build a bounded action queue before play. Useful steps include exact catalog inspection, seeded
   dice, clock advancement, current-scene movement, scene-affordance publication, location-visit
   capture, chapter advancement, conversation turns, and final readback.
4. Resolve each player-like instruction through `system.interaction-plan` operation `resolve` first.
   Inspect only trusted search results. If the Inner AI fails, inspect the exact active contract,
   submit a caller-built proposal through operation `submit`, and record immutable feedback.
5. Execute only the exact verified proposal and receipt. Use a distinct idempotency key per action.
   A successful earlier action remains committed if a later action fails.
6. Use `dnd2024.mechanic.dice` for genuinely uncertain narrative checks when no character sheet is
   available. Do not claim an ability modifier, proficiency, DC-based success, damage, combat turn,
   spell, class feature, or inventory capability without the required D&D components.
7. Move scenes only with `dnd2024.mechanic.campaign.current-scene.set`, binding the campaign, its
   exact World, and a contained active location. The action creates campaign relevance when needed.
8. After entering a location, use `dnd2024.mechanic.campaign.location-visit.record` with the exact
   World clock. Publish new affordances separately; they are suggestions, not simulated choices.
9. Close and replace the active chapter atomically with
   `dnd2024.mechanic.campaign.chapter.advance`. Closing summaries contain only outcomes established
   by successful actions or durable conversation, and the new chapter stays in the same active arc.
10. Persist word-for-word user and assistant messages through the durable play conversation. Store
    only justified truths and exact trusted entity references. If the conversation context is stale,
    do not weaken reference validation; record the defect and continue only through verified state.
11. Finish with exact reads of the campaign, chapters, current scene, visits, World clock, party,
    conversation history, known truths, and every failed receipt.

## Constraints

- Machine simulation may choose actions but must label them as simulated, not user-authored.
- World facts and GM context are not automatically player knowledge.
- Dice are evidence of a roll, not permission to invent unsupported D&D calculations.
- Completing a chapter does not resolve its arc, end its session, or create a quest.
- Recipes cannot retain literal mechanic input. Prefer parameter-free verified recipes; otherwise
  teach through active action contracts and procedures.
