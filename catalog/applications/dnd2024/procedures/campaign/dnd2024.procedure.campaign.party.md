## Description

The campaign party is the deterministic projection of structurally valid active character
participations. It is not a separate mutable roster entity. This contract teaches an AI how to
form and inspect that party without introducing a second source of truth.

## Instructions

1. Orient first, then read `procedure.system.use`, this contract, and
   `procedure.campaign.character-participation`. Resolve the host-bound application and state space;
   never accept an overlay profile or invent an application binding.
2. Inspect the exact campaign root, its `game.core.campaign.in-world` relationship, its current
   scene, and every `game.core.campaign.has-character-participation` edge. A member counts only
   when the participation component is active and exactly one
   `game.core.campaign.character-participation.for-actor` edge reaches an existing actor.
3. Do not create `party.*`, `party.root`, a party member list component, or a duplicate campaign
   roster. Websites and AI derive the roster from active participations in stable actor-name and ID
   order.
4. To add an existing actor, inspect the actor exactly and use
   `dnd2024.mechanic.campaign.character.attach`. Supply the exact campaign and actor roles and the
   campaign-owned participation ID. Never change the actor while attaching it.
5. In an explicitly authorized machine simulation, a narrative companion may be created with
   `dnd2024.mechanic.campaign.companion.create-and-attach`. Bind the exact campaign, its World, and
   its exact current location. The action creates one actor, traveller state, party-visible motive,
   presence, and participation atomically.
6. A narrative companion is not a completed D&D character. Do not claim abilities, class, species,
   hit points, equipment, spells, or rules behavior until the D&D character-creation owner has
   created those records. Report `characterSheetStatus: not-created` honestly.
7. Form a multi-member party as one verified action per member. Read each resolution receipt before
   execution, use a distinct idempotency key, and read back the actor, participation, and campaign
   graph after every successful commit. A later failure does not roll back earlier members.
8. Resume play only after the exact roster has been verified. Persist word-for-word conversation,
   current situation participants and location, and only justified truths. Participant IDs must be
   exact actor IDs from the verified roster.
9. If automatic intent cannot find this contract or either action, inspect the effective catalog
   record exactly and submit immutable feedback with the failed receipt and search evidence. Do not
   fabricate a successful plan or treat a catalog file as active runtime evidence.
10. Withdrawal uses the existing campaign character-participation lifecycle. Disabled, withdrawn,
    deleted, malformed, or duplicate participation graphs do not count as party members.

## Constraints

- Party membership is campaign scope, not player ownership, authentication, character knowledge,
  current location, encounter participation, or faction membership.
- The companion action is for machine-simulation or reviewed narrative identities. It cannot stand
  in for the D&D character-creation workflow.
- Every party-visible response and media projection remains subject to the authenticated audience.
- Exact qualified inspection remains available to authorized operators, including withdrawn or
  malformed participation evidence.
