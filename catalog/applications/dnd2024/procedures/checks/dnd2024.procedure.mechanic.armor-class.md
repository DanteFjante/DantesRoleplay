## Description

Selects a source-owned defense basis and derives final Armor Class without storing the result.

## Instructions

Resolve the creature's `armorClassSource`, validate its declared calculation, and run that
calculation against authoritative creature state. Ordinary unarmored defense is `10 + Dexterity
modifier`; an entitled Monk who wears no armor and uses no Shield can select `10 + Dexterity
modifier + Wisdom modifier`.

## Constraints

- Callers cannot supply final Armor Class, ability scores, modifiers, or a replacement source.
- Armor, Shields, other class-specific formulas, cover, and temporary effects require their own
  declared defense sources or later modifier owners.
