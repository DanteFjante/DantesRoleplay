## Description

Owns explicit encounter round and turn entities after locked participant Initiative exists.

## Instructions

1. Start creates caller-identified round 1 and turn ordinal 0, their ownership relationships, active
   relationships, and the turn's fresh counted budget in one transaction.
2. Advance completes the active turn, creates the next caller-identified turn and budget, and either
   retains the active round or completes it and creates the next round when Initiative wraps.
3. Derive participant order solely from active encounter participation and locked Initiative. End
   completes the active turn and round and removes both active relationships.

## Constraints

- Never re-roll or modify Initiative, mutate prior turn budgets during lifecycle transitions, or use
  encounter containment after participation has been established.
- Duplicate runtime IDs, participation/order drift, corrupt history, and invalid active state fail
  without effects.
- Reaction windows, position/movement execution, victory detection, reset, and restart remain out of scope.
