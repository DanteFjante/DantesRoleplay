## Description

Projects and changes authoritative square-grid encounter state without moving D&D rules into the
website. Board art may be attached as reviewed encounter media, but pixels never establish grid
geometry, collision, range, visibility, or movement cost.

## Instructions

Use `dnd2024.query.encounter-board` for the current audience-safe board. It returns no effects and
omits concealed terrain, obstacles, and positions from Player results without exposing their count.

Use `dnd2024.mechanic.encounter.board.place` to establish or correct a participation position with
exact board and position revisions. Use `dnd2024.mechanic.encounter.board.move` only for the active
participation. Supply the exact adjacent path and its terrain-adjusted metric spend; the pinned
turn-budget child rechecks Speed, Conditions, active turn, and remaining movement before the
position and budget commit atomically.

The website renders only returned grid facts and mechanic previews. It must not infer movement
legality, collision, range, hidden identities, or turn ownership from pixels or local React state.

## Constraints

- Grid dimensions are at most 64 by 64 and footprints at most 8 by 8.
- Placement and movement fail closed on stale board, position, or active-turn evidence.
- Generic containment remains the owner of world location.
- Ordinary wheel scrolling never changes tactical-board zoom.
