## Description

Owns effect-free profile-defined damage dice for a confirmed normal or critical weapon hit.

## Instructions

Accept exactly permitted ability and critical Boolean. Double base dice on critical, add the ability
modifier once, clamp below zero, and return ordered rolls and damage.

## Constraints

This does not confirm the hit, add PB, persist outcomes, apply damage, or own mitigation, temporary
HP, Conditions, death, equipment, range, or extra damage.
