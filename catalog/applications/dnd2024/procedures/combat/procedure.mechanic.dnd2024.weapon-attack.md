## Description

Owns a seeded effect-free weapon attack against authoritative Armor Class.

## Instructions

Read abilities, total level, weapon category proficiencies, selected profile, and target AC. Accept
only a profile-permitted str/dex ability; derive modifier/PB, roll one d20, and report hit/critical.

## Constraints

Natural 20 hits/criticals and natural 1 misses. This current owner has no range, Conditions,
Advantage, equipment, custody, damage, HP, or persisted attack state.
