## Description

Composes exact pace and duration resolution, an authored conditional navigation check, pending
exposure scheduling, generic route movement, journey recording, arrival, and authoritative clock
advancement. All child mechanics are pinned and the complete proposal commits atomically.

## Matches

travel the party along the named route
take this route to the destination
resolve overland travel and arrive
journey from the current location to the destination
