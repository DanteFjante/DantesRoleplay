## Description

Projects one source-bound page of the selected World's people, creatures, and DM-only holdings.
Declared containment owns World and location membership; motive, creature, item, conveyance, and
gate components own classification. The result reports when the generic containment depth omitted
deeper records instead of presenting a depth-limited snapshot as a complete World directory.

## Matches

page people in the selected world
page dm holdings by location
