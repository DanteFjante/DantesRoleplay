## Description

Applies one already-established damage amount through the object's authored threshold and response
profile. Reaching zero requires the exact destruction child input; a special resilient item remains
at zero but not destroyed until its separate authored requirement can be verified.

## Matches

damage an object
damage an item trap vehicle or scenery
