## Description

Resolves exactly one authored interval. A selected mitigation names one authored mitigation index;
otherwise the exact authored check is delegated to the verified ability-check mechanic. Prepared
damage and Condition consequences must byte-match the definition and verified outcome.

## Matches

resolve an environmental exposure interval
apply an environmental hazard consequence
