## Description

Projects a bounded, audience-filtered list of poison, contagion, curse, and environmental-exposure
records already attached to one subject. Player projections omit concealed curses; the projection
never scans broad world state or invents an undiscovered hazard.

## Matches

show due hazards and afflictions
what poison contagion curse or exposure is active
show current hazard status
