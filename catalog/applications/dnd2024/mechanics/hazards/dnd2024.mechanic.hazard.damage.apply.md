## Description

Rolls one closed, authored hazard damage amount and applies the existing D&D damage-mitigation
profile before spending Temporary Hit Points and Hit Points. The parent trap or exposure mechanic
must prove that this exact consequence appears in the authored hazard definition.

## Matches

apply authored hazard damage
resolve trap or environmental damage
