## Description

Validates one closed prepared consequence list against the exact authored trap definition. Any
authored uncertainty runs through the verified saving-throw child; damage and Conditions remain
owned by their focused child mechanics. The trap phase, consequences, and event commit atomically.

## Matches

trigger an armed trap
resolve a trap consequence
