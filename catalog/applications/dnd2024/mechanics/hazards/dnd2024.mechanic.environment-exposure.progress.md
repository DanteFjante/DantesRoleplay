## Description

Composes one exact exposure-resolution interval with the authoritative world clock. The exposure
state, damage, Conditions, typed event, and clock movement form one transaction and one replay
lineage, so a retry never resolves or advances the interval twice.

## Matches

progress environmental exposure
advance one hazard interval
