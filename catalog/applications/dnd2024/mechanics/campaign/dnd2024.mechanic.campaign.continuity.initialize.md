## Description

Creates exactly one active opening arc and one active opening chapter for a campaign that has no
continuity yet.

## Matches

initialize campaign continuity
create the opening arc and chapter
prepare the first campaign chapter

## Requirements

```json
{"roles":{"campaign":{"components":["game.core.campaign.root"],"includeRelationships":true,"description":"The active campaign root whose continuity has not been initialized."}},"effectComponentIds":["game.core.campaign.arc","game.core.campaign.chapter"],"inputSchema":{"type":"object","additionalProperties":false,"required":["arcId","arcTitle","partyStake","arcGmContext","chapterId","chapterTitle","partyQuestion","chapterGmContext"],"properties":{"arcId":{"type":"string","minLength":1,"maxLength":200},"arcTitle":{"type":"string","minLength":1,"maxLength":160},"partyStake":{"type":"string","minLength":1,"maxLength":500},"arcGmContext":{"type":"string","minLength":1,"maxLength":1000},"chapterId":{"type":"string","minLength":1,"maxLength":200},"chapterTitle":{"type":"string","minLength":1,"maxLength":160},"partyQuestion":{"type":"string","minLength":1,"maxLength":500},"chapterGmContext":{"type":"string","minLength":1,"maxLength":1000}}}}
```

## Input

Supply exactly `arcId`, `arcTitle`, `partyStake`, `arcGmContext`, `chapterId`, `chapterTitle`,
`partyQuestion`, and `chapterGmContext`. Child IDs must be beneath the campaign ID.
