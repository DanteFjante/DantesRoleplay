## Description

Creates exactly one active opening arc and one active opening chapter for a campaign that has no
continuity yet.

## Matches

initialize campaign continuity
create the opening arc and chapter
prepare the first campaign chapter

## Requirements

```json
{"roles":{"campaign":{"components":["game.core.campaign.root"],"includeRelationships":true,"description":"The active campaign root whose continuity has not been initialized."}},"effectComponentIds":["game.core.campaign.arc","game.core.campaign.chapter"]}
```

## Input

Supply exactly `arcId`, `arcTitle`, `partyStake`, `arcGmContext`, `chapterId`, `chapterTitle`,
`partyQuestion`, and `chapterGmContext`. Child IDs must be beneath the campaign ID.
