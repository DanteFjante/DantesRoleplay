## Description

Projects the complete bounded chapter, arc, and session structure attached to one authorized
campaign. Player projection omits GM context and session records. The projection reads only
declared campaign relationships and never scans or classifies the entity directory.

## Matches

show campaign details
show campaign chapters and arcs
show campaign adventure history
