## Description

Projects the bounded union of knowledge admitted to active members of one exact authorized
campaign. The host supplies one immutable graph snapshot; the mechanic derives its World and
party from declared relationships and never accepts an actor, World, audience, or visibility
override as input. Familiar-only recognition remains identity-free. A record with no unique
safe display subject retains its independently authorized knowledge fields, reports partial
field coverage, and omits subject and location grouping rather than choosing or exposing one.
Continuation cursors advance over the exact consumed source prefix, so a bounded response never
skips a fact merely because a full host page would exceed the transport budget.
