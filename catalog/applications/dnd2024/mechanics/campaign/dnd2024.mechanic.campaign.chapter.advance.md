## Description

Atomically closes one exact active campaign chapter with a factual summary and creates its next
active chapter inside the same active arc. It preserves prior chapters as immutable continuity.

## Matches

advance to the next campaign chapter
finish the current chapter and begin another
record a chapter outcome and continue the story
continue the campaign after completing a chapter

## Requirements

```json
{"roles":{"campaign":{"components":["game.core.campaign.root"],"includeRelationships":true,"description":"The active campaign that owns both chapters and the arc."},"arc":{"components":["game.core.campaign.arc"],"includeRelationships":true,"description":"The exact active arc receiving the next chapter."},"chapter":{"components":["game.core.campaign.chapter"],"includeRelationships":true,"description":"The exact active chapter being closed."}},"effectComponentIds":["game.core.campaign.chapter"]}
```

## Input

Supply exactly `nextChapterId`, `nextTitle`, `nextPartyQuestion`, `nextGmContext`, and
`closingSummary`. The next ID must be a new child beneath the campaign's `.chapter.` namespace.
