## Description

Completes a duration-ready activity against its still-current definition. Crafting delegates its
single authored physical output to the existing item-instance creator; all other families record
mechanical completion without inventing a narrative result. Child failure rolls back completion.

## Matches

complete downtime
finish crafting recovery service training or lifestyle downtime
