## Description

Starts one exact crafting, recovery, service, training, or lifestyle activity. The immutable
definition supplies duration, prerequisite keys, reservation totals, and cancellation behavior.
Selected physical currency and material stacks are validated beneath the participant and consumed
exactly once in the same transaction. This mechanic records no creative outcome.

## Matches

begin a downtime activity
start crafting recovery service training or lifestyle downtime
