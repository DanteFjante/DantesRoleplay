## Description

Adds a bounded interval to one active downtime activity and advances the authoritative world clock
in the same transaction. Progress stops exactly at the declared duration and becomes ready, not
implicitly complete.

## Matches

progress downtime
work on crafting recovery training service or lifestyle activity
