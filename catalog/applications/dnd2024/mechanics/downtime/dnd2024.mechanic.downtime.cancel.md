## Description

Cancels an active or ready activity only when its pinned definition permits it. Retain consumes no
new effects. Refund requires one explicit replacement ID per consumed reservation and recreates the
exact definition, quantity, name, and slot recorded at start.

## Matches

cancel downtime
cancel and refund a downtime activity
