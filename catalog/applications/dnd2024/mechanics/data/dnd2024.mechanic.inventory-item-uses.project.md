## Description

Projects exact linked activity records and authorized statements for one carried
instance. Linked raw records require knowledge of that activity itself; knowing
a statement does not reveal its costs or hidden effects. Inline fixed activities
retain their existing DM-only raw boundary. Names never infer activities.
Literal supported fields are descriptive; knowledge, execution support and
eligibility remain separate. No mechanic is invoked and no resource is consumed.
Missing dependencies stay partial. Stable pages require the authorized revision.
