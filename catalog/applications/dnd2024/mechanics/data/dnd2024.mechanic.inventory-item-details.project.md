## Description

Returns a read-only dossier for one bounded, observer-authorized inventory instance.
Instance identity, quantity, equipment and durability retain their own owners; physical
and equipment-definition fields come from the linked definition, as in inventory.read
and item-burden.read. No blanket instance/definition merge or new rule calculation occurs.
Player identity and definition facets require confirmed effective knowledge; uncertain
statements retain their stance without granting the rest of a definition. Unidentified
items use a neutral label. Raw private facets remain DM-only; Player statements come
only from the host-authorized knowledge source. Missing required definitions fail closed.
Reference-only rules, charges and unsupported predicates are unavailable dependencies,
never inferred values. Media uses host-issued, view-bound links with instance-first
roles and permitted definition illustrations/icons after the same identity checks.
No executable actions are returned.

## Matches

inspect one authorized carried item dossier
