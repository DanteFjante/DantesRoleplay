## Description

Spends caller-selected Hit Point Dice for a ready Short Rest or recovers every spent Hit Point Die
for a ready Long Rest. Pool maxima derive from exact class-membership levels.

## Matches

spend hit point dice after a short rest
recover hit point dice after a long rest
