## Description

Records one complete reviewed Magic Initiate choice set after the caller binds two distinct
cantrips and one level-1 spell from the entitlement's exact spell list and chooses Intelligence,
Wisdom, or Charisma. It never chooses spells or an ability for the character.

## Matches

configure magic initiate
record magic initiate spell choices
