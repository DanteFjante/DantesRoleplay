## Description

Matches exact output and material definition references for one carried instance.
Only host-authorized recipe records and effective knowledge are used. Known
incomplete candidates stay partial; missing reference labels remain unavailable.
Supported literal durations and single-reference proficiency requirements are
described without evaluating eligibility. Parameterized recipes and unsupported
predicates remain incomplete or unavailable dependencies. Availability is never
inferred from possessions, names or prose. No crafting action or effects execute.
Each group pages independently over authorized matches in stable identity order.
Continuation requires the same authorized source revision; stale pages fail closed.
