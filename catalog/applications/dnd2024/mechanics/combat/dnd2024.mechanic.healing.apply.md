## Description
Applies one positive healing amount to a creature's current Hit Points, capped at its existing
maximum. Temporary Hit Points are owned by a separate component and are not changed.

## Matches
heal the character
heal hit points
restore hit points
apply healing

## Requirements
```json
{"roles":{"subject":{"components":["dnd2024.creature.hit-points"],"description":"The creature whose authoritative Hit Points receive healing."}},"inputSchema":{"type":"object","additionalProperties":false,"required":["amount"],"properties":{"amount":{"type":"integer","minimum":1,"maximum":2147483647}}}}
```

## Input and result
Provide exactly `{"amount": <positive integer>}`. The result reports requested, applied, and
overflow amounts. It proposes one `component.set` only when Hit Points increase; healing at the
existing maximum is a successful no-change result. Malformed or invalid Hit Point state fails.
