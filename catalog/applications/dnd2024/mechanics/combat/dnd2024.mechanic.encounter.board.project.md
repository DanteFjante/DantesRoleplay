## Description

Projects one authoritative encounter grid, visible terrain and obstacles, exact positioned
participants, Initiative order, and active turn. The host supplies the trusted Player or DM
audience. Concealed board records are omitted without revealing their identity or count.

## Matches

show the tactical encounter board
show combat positions and obstacles
where is everyone in this encounter
