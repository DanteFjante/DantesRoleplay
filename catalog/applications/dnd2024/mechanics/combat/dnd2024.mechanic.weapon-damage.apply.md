## Description

Consumes one declared weapon-damage child result and atomically spends optional target Temporary
Hit Points before current Hit Points. Positive damage also validates and interrupts any active
canonical rest episode in the same transaction; damage absorbed by Temporary Hit Points still
counts, while immunity and duration-ready rests do not interrupt.

## Matches

apply weapon damage
damage target with weapon
