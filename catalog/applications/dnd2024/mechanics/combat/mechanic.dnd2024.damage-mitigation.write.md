## Description

Records or corrects a creature's complete known D&D 2024 Resistance, Immunity, and Vulnerability
type memberships. It does not resolve damage or change Hit Points.

## Matches

record damage resistances
record damage immunities
record damage vulnerabilities
set damage mitigation
correct damage mitigation
