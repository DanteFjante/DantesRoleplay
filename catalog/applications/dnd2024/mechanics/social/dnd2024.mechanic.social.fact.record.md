## Description

Records one bounded reason or campaign consequence exactly as supplied by the DM or authorized AI.
It creates no dialogue, attitude, intention, acceptance, or future NPC action.

## Matches

record a social reason
record a campaign social consequence

## Requirements

```json
{"roles":{"source":{"components":[],"description":"The exact actor whose selected attitude is being explained."},"target":{"components":[],"description":"The exact actor or faction the selected attitude concerns."},"campaign":{"components":["game.core.campaign.root"],"optional":true,"description":"The campaign owner required only for a consequence fact."}},"effectComponentIds":["game.core.world.fact"],"inputSchema":{"type":"object","additionalProperties":false,"required":["factId","kind","summary","provenance","visibility"],"properties":{"factId":{"type":"string","minLength":1,"maxLength":200},"kind":{"enum":["reason","consequence"]},"summary":{"type":"string","minLength":1,"maxLength":1000},"provenance":{"type":"string","minLength":1,"maxLength":500},"visibility":{"enum":["party","gm"]}}},"elapsedTime":{"mode":"zero"}}
```

## Input

Supply the permanent fact ID, selected summary, provenance, visibility, and whether this is a
current-attitude reason or a separate campaign consequence. A consequence also requires `campaign`.

## Output

Creates one governed generic fact and exact relationship links. Returned data contains identifiers
and visibility only; private prose is not copied into receipts.
