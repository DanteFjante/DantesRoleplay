## Description

Persists an attitude transition selected by the DM or authorized AI. It validates exact
participants, current revision, the closed D&D attitude vocabulary, explicit reasons, and optional
receipt evidence. It never evaluates persuasion or chooses what an NPC thinks, says, accepts, or
does.

## Matches

record an npc attitude change
set a social attitude toward someone
record the consequence of a negotiation

## Input

The caller explicitly supplies the old and selected next attitude, exact record ID, visibility,
bounded authored reason facts, optional verified receipt references, and the expected record
revision. An unchanged attitude is a no-op only when no new reasons, evidence, consequence, or
visibility change is requested.

## Output

Creates or replaces one social-attitude component and emits `dnd2024.social.attitude-changed` in
the same transaction. Receipt-safe data contains no reason prose, dialogue, intent, acceptance, or
future NPC action.
