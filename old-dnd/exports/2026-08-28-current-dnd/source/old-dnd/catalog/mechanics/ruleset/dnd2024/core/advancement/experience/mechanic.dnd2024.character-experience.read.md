## Description

Reads a character's explicit experience and total level as diagnostics, deriving only whether XP
has reached the fixed threshold for the exact next level. It has no effects and is safe for later
campaign authorization and character-level-up composition.

## Matches

inspect character experience
read character experience eligibility
