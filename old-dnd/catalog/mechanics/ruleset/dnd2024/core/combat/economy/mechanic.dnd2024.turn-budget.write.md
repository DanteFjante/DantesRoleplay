## Description

Records or corrects the complete D&D 2024 turn-resource budget for one participant. Administrative
state only: it neither refreshes nor spends a resource, and it never decides whether another rule
costs an Action, Bonus Action, Reaction, interaction, or movement.

## Matches

record turn budget
correct turn budget
record action-economy budget
