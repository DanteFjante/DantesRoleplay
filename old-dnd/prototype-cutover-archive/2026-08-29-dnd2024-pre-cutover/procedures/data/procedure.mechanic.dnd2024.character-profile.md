## Description

Records or corrects optional descriptive pronouns, appearance, biography, and player-facing notes
for an existing actor.

## Instructions

Use explicit `record` for absent state or `correct` for valid existing state. Supply at least one
trimmed nonblank identity field and no other keys. Use the prototype field limits: 200 characters
for pronouns, 5,000 for appearance, and 20,000 for biography or player notes. Each success adds or
sets exactly one component.

## Constraints

This record does not establish display name, campaign membership, visibility, control, permission,
character-creation completion, or any game rule. Those checks must be composed by their owners.
